About the lab

ATLAS AI Lab at Baylor College of Medicine studies artificial intelligence and machine learning for healthcare. We bring computational methods and clinical questions together to develop useful, interpretable, and responsible AI.

Led by Muhammad Aurangzeb Ahmad, Assistant Professor at Baylor College of Medicine.

Research directions

Learning from clinical data

Methods for temporal and heterogeneous healthcare data, including dynamic risk prediction and patient trajectories.

Interpretable & responsible AI

Understanding model behavior, evaluating fairness, and building reliable systems for clinical use.

Causal AI & decision support

Studying interventions and developing computational approaches to support healthcare decisions.
